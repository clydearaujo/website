AI Skill · Human In The Loop Kahneman Council: System 1 and 2 reasoning, built in Daniel Kahneman showed that fast, intuitive judgement feels right and is often wrong. AI doesn't fix that: models often drop a correct answer after one challenge (Sharma et al., 2023). In this skill, five reviewers judge a decision independently. A Chair modelled on Thinking, Fast and Slow then checks their reasoning for anchoring, sunk cost and overconfidence. I set the assumptions and make the final call.

Zalando's own AI agent will buy from partner shops

AI Agents · Retail Media · Loyalty
What It Means

Zalando announced Zalando Extended with its Q3 results on 3 November. It adds products from other online shops to the Zalando app. From 2027, Zalando's own AI agent checks them out for customers. Westwing is the first pilot partner. Partners can also buy retail media on Zalando. Zalando Plus has more than 22 million members. Zalando

My VerdictWatch

Shoppers will buy your products without visiting your shop. My bet: the order data becomes Zalando's next product. If you sell through Zalando, agree before 2027 who books the sale, whose loyalty points members earn and who owns customer data. Ecommerce and finance: judge it on margin and repeat customers.

EU court adviser: data-funded 'free' offers may mislead

Consumer Law · Loyalty · CJEU
What It Means

On 19 November, Advocate General Medina advised the EU Court of Justice in a German case over Meta's "Facebook is and remains free". In her view, personal data given for commercial use count as a cost, so a "free" claim can be an unfair practice in all circumstances. GDPR compliance does not settle it. The opinion is not binding. EU Court of Justice (PDF)

My VerdictAct Now

Members join a loyalty programme for the rewards and expect an honest deal. If their data pays for offers or retail media, "free" may count as misleading. Have legal and CRM list every place you say it, rewrite it to state the exchange, then track whether sign-ups hold.

AI lifted sales most where shoppers had no help

AI ROI · Experiments · Ecommerce
What It Means

Researchers from Columbia and Zhejiang University analysed AI tests at an online retailer, each against a group without AI. The biggest win, 16.3% more sales, came from an AI chat assistant answering questions before purchase, where shoppers used to get no reply. AI-improved search and product texts added 2% to 3%. AI-written ads and marketing messages made no measurable difference. Working paper (arXiv)

My VerdictPilot It

A shopper whose question goes unanswered is a sale at risk. If your shop leaves questions open, for example outside service hours, start AI there: in this study, that gap held the biggest gain. Have your ecommerce team test it against a control group and judge it on extra sales.
