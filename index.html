Salesforce moves to acquire Braze

Agentic CRM · Customer engagement · October 2026
What it means

Salesforce is paying $5.7 billion for an embedded engagement engine instead of leaning on composable CDPs. Its customers now have overlapping tools for journeys, decisioning and sending, and Braze users lose a vendor they used as leverage in price talks. MarTech

My verdict

I'd lock in your Braze or Marketing Cloud renewal terms now, while you still have two vendors to play off against each other. And keep one independent orchestration vendor on the shortlist for your next evaluation.

Databricks puts agents inside its customer data platform

Customer data architecture · AI plumbing · October 2026
What it means

On 19 October Databricks moved Campaign Agents, which build audiences and run campaigns, into beta inside its CustomerLake CDP. Profile Agents, which resolve customer identities with rule-based, probabilistic and LLM matching, are in private preview. CMSWire

My verdict

If your customer data already sits in Databricks, test this on one audience. Matching on email or a login ID is the easy part. Check a sample of the probabilistic and LLM matches by hand, because one wrongly merged profile sends the wrong offer to the wrong person. If your data lives somewhere else, I wouldn't move it for this.

AI-generated content needs marking by 2 December

EU data & AI rules · AI Act Article 50
What it means

Under the EU's Digital Omnibus, providers of generative AI systems already on the market before 2 August 2026 have until 2 December 2026 to meet the AI Act's marking rules for synthetic text, images, audio and video. High-risk obligations for CV screening and employment decisions, credit scoring, education access, critical infrastructure and law enforcement are pending until December 2027. Orrick

My verdict

Before 2 December, ask every GenAI vendor in your content stack how they mark what they generate, and get the answer in writing. Marking is their legal duty, but if an unmarked campaign goes out, it's your brand people will talk about.
