5 November 2026

People say they'd pay for AI transparency

Consumer Trust · AI Personalisation · 2026 Research
What It Means

Usercentrics surveyed 11,000 consumers in seven markets: 52% would pay more for transparency about AI, 73% in Germany. 71% find AI-driven personalisation intrusive. 52% also trust AI less than humans with their personal data, up from 48% in 2025. Usercentrics sells consent tools, so read the numbers as a direction. Usercentrics

My VerdictPilot It

What people say in surveys isn't always what they do, so test it first. Add one line to one personalised journey on why the customer sees this offer. Run it against the current version for a month and compare opt-outs and conversion. If you sell in Germany, start there.

The weak spot in human in the loop is the human

AI Governance · Agent Oversight · 2026
What It Means

Jason Bloomberg of Intellyx calls human-in-the-loop oversight “fundamentally flawed” once agents scale. He proposes automation in the loop: agents assist, people decide. The EU’s own Ethics Guidelines for Trustworthy AI say a human in every decision is often “neither possible nor desirable”. High-risk AI rules, human oversight among them, apply from 2 December 2027. SiliconANGLE · EU Ethics Guidelines · Mondaq

My VerdictAct Now

An approval that takes two seconds is a click. I keep mine few and slow: one entry, its sources, every fact checked before I sign. Time a week of your team's approvals. If each takes seconds, you have a log and the risk is still yours.

Human in the loop, on the loop or in command?

AI Governance · Oversight Models · EU Guidance
What It Means

The EU’s Ethics Guidelines for Trustworthy AI separate three kinds of oversight. In the loop, a person intervenes in every decision. On the loop, a person monitors live operation. In command, a person decides whether and how to deploy. Article 14 of the AI Act also warns against automation bias. EU Ethics Guidelines · AI Act Art. 14

My VerdictPilot It

Most teams say “human in the loop” without saying which kind. Name it for each AI workflow touching customer data and warn reviewers the system can be wrong. In a 2023 study, warned people checked more and checking went with better decisions. For Signals, I chose in the loop.

Salesforce moves to acquire Braze

Agentic CRM · Customer Engagement · October 2026
What It Means

Salesforce is paying $5.7 billion for an embedded engagement engine instead of leaning on composable CDPs. Its customers now have overlapping tools for journeys, decisioning and sending. Gartner expects Salesforce to retire one of its four marketing execution engines by 2029, pushing over 30% of affected customers into unplanned replatforming. MarTech

My VerdictWatch

I'd lock in your Braze or Marketing Cloud renewal terms now, while you still have two vendors to play off against each other. Keep one independent orchestration vendor on the shortlist for your next evaluation and map the workflows and rules you'd need to move if you left.

Databricks puts agents inside its customer data platform

Customer Data Architecture · AI Plumbing · October 2026
What It Means

On 19 October Databricks moved Campaign Agents, which build audiences and run campaigns, into beta inside its CustomerLake CDP. Profile Agents, which resolve customer identities with rule-based, probabilistic and LLM matching, are in private preview. Early customers report fast results: Wine.com, for one, ran production email campaigns 23 days after kickoff. CMSWire

My VerdictPilot It

If your customer data already sits in Databricks, test this on one audience. Check a sample of the probabilistic and LLM matches by hand, because one wrongly merged profile sends the wrong offer to the wrong person. If your data lives somewhere else, I wouldn't move it for this.

AI-generated content needs marking by 2 December

EU Data & AI Rules · AI Act Article 50
What It Means

Under the EU's Digital Omnibus, providers of generative AI systems already on the market before 2 August 2026 have until 2 December 2026 to meet the AI Act's marking rules for synthetic text, images, audio and video. High-risk obligations for CV screening and employment decisions, credit scoring, education access, critical infrastructure and law enforcement are pending until December 2027. Orrick

My VerdictAct Now

Before 2 December, ask every GenAI vendor in your content stack how they mark what they generate and get the answer in writing. Marking is their legal duty, but if an unmarked campaign goes out, it's your brand people will talk about.
