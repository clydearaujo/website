Henkel · Schwarzkopf Professional SalonLab · B2B2C IoT · 2022 to 2026

Connecting the salon chair to the shopping basket

An offline-to-online use case with IoT as the enabler. After a fast acquisition integration, I reset the foundations of a two-sided platform and built it for growth, while running the CRM side.

+15%First-time conversion
+20%Repeat purchase

Context

Schwarzkopf Professional introduced SalonLab, its connected hair-analysis system for salons, in 2018 (Happi). In 2022 Henkel launched SalonLab&Me, its first B2B2C hyper-personalised professional hair care brand (Henkel).

The situation

SalonLab came into Schwarzkopf through an acquisition and was integrated quickly into existing systems built for a different operating model. The three main data sets needed harmonising: device readings, product data and consumer profiles had ended up in separate places, so a consumer's in-salon diagnosis didn't carry through to what they bought next.

What I did

  • Pressed reset: rather than patching the integration, I cleaned up the foundations the platform needed to operate reliably.
  • Harmonised one B2B2C data model linking salon devices, product data and consumer profiles to e-commerce.
  • Took the platform to market, connecting salon diagnostics to personalised journeys built on BigQuery and GenAI workflows.
  • Started implementing customer unit-economics (LTV:CAC) tracking and the measurement architecture for the platform.
  • Ran the CRM side throughout, so the reset turned into growth rather than a pause.

The result

First-time conversion rose by 15% and repeat purchase by 20%, on foundations built to keep growing.
