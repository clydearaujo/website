Henkel · Schwarzkopf Professional SalonLab · B2B2C IoT

Connecting the salon chair to the shopping basket

One data model for a two-sided platform: the salon on one side, the consumer on the other, and e-commerce in between.

Context

Schwarzkopf Professional introduced SalonLab, its connected hair-analysis system for salons, in 2018 (Happi). In 2022 Henkel launched SalonLab&Me, which it described as its first B2B2C hyper-personalised professional hair care brand (Henkel).

The problem

Device readings, product data and consumer profiles sat in separate systems. A consumer's in-salon diagnosis did not carry through to what they bought next.

What I did

  • Led the harmonisation of one B2B2C data model linking salon devices, product data and consumer profiles to e-commerce.
  • Took the platform to market, connecting salon diagnostics to personalised journeys built on BigQuery, GenAI workflows and Azure AI.
