From audit to €14M: rebuilding a global CRM programme

I ran the audit that made the case for change, wrote the business case that funded it, and led the delivery.

The situation

Reporting into Henkel's Chief Digital Office, I led a global audit of CRM and consumer data across two consumer categories and several mature markets. It found data assets that were costly to run but generated no revenue, and fragmented data across acquisition and retention funnels. Senior management agreed the programme had to change.

What I did

  • Moved to the consumer business unit's global team and wrote the business case that secured €14M: €12M CapEx to select and implement a new tech stack, plus €2M for programme marketing.
  • As business owner of Salesforce Marketing Cloud, led the decision, based on that business case, to move the stack to Adobe.
  • Led delivery as Head of Global eCRM, with a multi-country team of up to 14 specialists and managers across CRM technology, lifecycle marketing and performance analysis.
  • Owned the CRM targets: lifetime value, retention, engagement and member growth.
  • Created repeatable rollout playbooks that took the platform into 12 international markets, with local activation under one global governance model.
  • Unified 5M+ consumer profiles through first-party data capture and retail data clean-room collaborations under GDPR, reaching a 1:4 CAC to LTV ratio.
  • Was accountable for tagging in BigQuery and GA4, so the programme could be measured.

How I worked

My team operated the platforms. I owned the programme, the stakeholders and the strategy, and went into the detailed execution with the team where it mattered.

The result

In two years, in one large, mature European market, the consumer base grew from 2 million to 5 million and the programme delivered +25% incremental net external sales. Across all markets, the return was on track for positive incremental net sales over the seven years of the €14M business case.
