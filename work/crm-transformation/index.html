Henkel · Head of Global eCRM

From audit to €14M: rebuilding a global CRM programme

I ran the audit while in the Chief Digital Office team that made the case for change, wrote the business case that funded it, and led the delivery.

The situation

Reporting into Henkel's Chief Digital Office, I led a global audit of CRM and consumer data across two consumer categories and several mature markets. It found fragmented data assets globally, with varying operating models and no tangible connection to revenue. Senior management agreed the programme had to change.

What I did

  • Moved to the consumer business unit's global team and wrote the business case that secured €14M: €12M CapEx to select and implement a new tech stack, plus €2M for programme marketing.
  • As business owner of the existing marketing platform, led the decision, based on that business case, to move the stack to a top-tier platform.
  • Led delivery as Head of Global eCRM, with a cross-functional team across countries of up to 14 specialists and managers across CRM technology, lifecycle marketing and performance analysis.
  • Owned the CRM targets: lifetime value, retention, engagement and member growth.
  • Created repeatable rollout playbooks and a centre of excellence that took the platform into 12 international markets, with local activation under one global governance model.
  • Unified 5M+ consumer profiles through first-party data capture and retail data clean-room collaborations under GDPR, reaching a 1:4 CAC to LTV ratio.
  • Was accountable for tagging in BigQuery and GA4, so the programme could be measured. It proved an effective quick win to harmonise and activate the data, before the work moved to the new platform.

How I worked

My team operated the platforms. I owned the programme, the stakeholders and the strategy, and went into the detailed execution with the team where it mattered. I built the team. I built the OGSM and got stakeholder buy-in. I created the roadmap for rolling out two operating models, and led the team through these tasks while securing the approvals and wins.

The result

In two years, the programme grew the consumer base from 2 million to 5 million. In the lead market, a large, mature European market, it delivered +25% incremental net external sales. Across all markets, the return was on track for positive incremental net sales over the seven years of the €14M business case.
